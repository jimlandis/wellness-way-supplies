How we deliver

From Factory to Your Facility

The Wellness Way Supplies handles the work between the factory and your facility:

  • Samples sent for approval before every production run
  • Third-party inspection before shipment
  • Warehouse storage of approved stock
  • Orders shipped out to Wellness Way facilities
  • Packaging developed for the brand (printed bags, color boxes or labels)
